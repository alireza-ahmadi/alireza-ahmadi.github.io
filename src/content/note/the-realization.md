i'm feeling **unbearable heaviness in my chest**. i _cried_ but it didn't help.
_walking? meditation? gaming? watching movies? donuts?_ none of them helped either.
decided to _talk with someone_ but had no luck so i pursued alternative options
and [settled on writing](https://x.com/david_perell/status/1424024555409092610?lang=en) this.

over the last year or so, i tried my best to [focus](https://alireza.es/notes/on-turning-30);
and i was fairly successful in it (_or at least, i think so_).
i disposed quite a few work-related threads, focused on my job which led to noticeable growth of the company that i'm working for.

however, during this period, **i didn't feel joy, not even a bit**.

**i'm not quite sure what is wrong**. _i assumed_ i felt lost because of decision fatigue,
therefore focusing on the most reliable option and cutting off other threads would
lead me to **what i want**. but what _exactly_ do i want?

i'm not quite sure what i want yet i'm pretty confident what i don't seek.
i mean, trying to focus for a while didn't lead to my desired outcome but it gave me an unexpected, yet fairly helpful, insight:
**i learned that i don't want a mediocre life**.

i don't want to be a middle manager. i don't want to be a random cog in a much larger machine.
i would **rather die than settle for a subpar end result**.
i don't want to do something just because _i should_ or because _i have no other options_.
i despise doing things for the sake of doing things.

for long, i tried to _be normal_, _act normal_, and _live a normal life_ like most people but time and time again
it has been proven to me that i'm not wired like that and probably **there is something wrong with me**. i don't want a normal life and
if i can't live my life the way i want, it's not worth living.

_am i delusional for thinking that i can control my life?_ maybe. but i have zero doubt that if i can't control
my life, i'd lose my mind; so it's a war worth fighting. it's worth mentioning tho, i don't expect to win every single battle but rather i want to win the war.
i only know that [i can shape things](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kYfNvmF0Bqw), and **i'd try to**.

when i started writing this, i didn't know what i wanted to write.
however, after spending a few hours writing, deleting, and writing again, i have a much better grasp of my inner feelings.
i still feel the heaviness in my chest but my thoughts are much more clear now.
